Who Are We?

Society of Open Science Students Moah (SOS) is a not-for-profit, student-led club dedicated to building a vibrant and inclusive community for science students at SOS University and beyond. Through exciting social events, networking opportunities, and student-focused activities, SOS helps students make the most of their university experience.

Know more

Our Mission

Our mission is to connect like-minded students throughout their academic journey, creating a strong sense of belonging and support within the university community. SOS aims to foster meaningful friendships, unforgettable experiences, and valuable industry connections that last well beyond university life.

Engaging Student Academic Events

Our plan is to create a welcoming and connected environment where science students feel supported, valued, and inspired throughout their university journey. Through sustainable initiatives, student-led academic experiences, social events, and meaningful networking opportunities, SOS aims to foster genuine connections, encourage collaboration, and strengthen the sense of belonging within the Moah science community. By empowering students to take an active role in shaping their university experience, we hope to build a lasting community that future students and committees can continue to grow for years to come.
